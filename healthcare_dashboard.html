1. Dataset Overview

This dataset contains information about people, their health conditions, healthcare support, and the number of times they visited a doctor. The purpose is to explore patterns and understand which factors may be associated with more (or fewer) doctor visits. Records: 5190, columns: 13.

2. Main Objective

The main objective is to analyze patient-related information and identify useful patterns in doctor visits. Patients can compare age, health condition, illness, income, chronic conditions, and healthcare support with the number of visits.

3. Column Definitions

  • visits — number of times the person visited a doctor during the recorded period (target measure).
  • gender — female / male (Personal Information).
  • age — age of the person (Personal Information).
  • income — income level (Financial Information).
  • illness — illness count/severity (Health Information).
  • reduced — reduced health flag (Health Information).
  • health — general health status (Health Information).
  • private — yes/no, private healthcare cover (Healthcare Support).
  • freepoor — yes/no, free/poor support (Healthcare Support).
  • freerepat — yes/no, free repatriation support (Healthcare Support).
  • nchronic / lchronic — yes/no chronic condition flags (Health Information).
  • id — record identifier (Record Identification).

4. Grouping the Columns

  • Personal Information: gender, age
  • Health Information: illness, reduced, health, nchronic, lchronic
  • Healthcare Support: private, freepoor, freerepat
  • Doctor Visit Information: visits
  • Record Identification: id

5. Simple Meaning of Healthcare Analytics

Healthcare analytics means studying healthcare-related data to find useful patterns and support better understanding and decision-making. Key learning outcome: patterns emerge when you compare patient attributes against visit counts.
